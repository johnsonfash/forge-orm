# The series — titles, order, tags, and cover-image prompts

Five posts, published in order as a dev.to **series**. The `series:` line in
each file's front matter is identical, which is what makes dev.to group them
and show "Part 1 of 5" navigation. Leave it exactly as it is.

Each file has `published: false`. Flip it to `true` when you're ready, or paste
the body into the dev.to editor and set the cover there.

**Suggested cadence:** one every 3–4 days. The series builds an argument, and
posting them all at once wastes the progression.

## Notes on the prompts

Every one ends with **no text, no logos, no readable writing**. Image models
render text as convincing gibberish, and gibberish on a cover reads as
low-effort faster than anything else on dev.to.

They are also all *scenes*, not diagrams. A cover image competes in a feed
against photographs; an abstract render of glowing hexagons and circuit lines
loses to a picture of a market stall at dusk every time.

If dev.to's generator gives you something flat, add one physical detail —
condensation, dust in a sunbeam, a chipped edge — and regenerate. Specific
imperfections are what stop these looking synthetic.
